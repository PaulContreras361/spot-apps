Put it on your home screen

  1. Open any app above in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Enter the family code once — it opens all three apps.
  3. You only need one home-screen button — the Apps tab on the right side switches between them.
  4. iPhone: tap Share (square with the arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
    Android: tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
